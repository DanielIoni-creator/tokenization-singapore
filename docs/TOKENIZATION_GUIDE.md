This guide explains how Singapore real estate properties are fractionalized and tokenized on-chain.

## Tokenization Process
1. **Property Appraisal:** Verification of property valuation in SGD/USD.
2. **SPV Formation:** Special Purpose Vehicle legal structure registration in Singapore.
3. **Smart Contract Deployment:** Minting ERC-20 compliant property tokens.
4. **Investor Onboarding:** KYC/AML verification and Monero (XMR) subscription payment.
